# W61 V6 preregistration — one YuNet 960 CPU hypothesis

This is a new, finite diagnostic after the sealed [V5 result](./w61-visual-preflight-v5-result.md), not a revision of the V5 corpus, labels, rights, selection, score or acceptance criteria. V5 development/calibration found 23 missed annotated faces: four of five faces with a minimum side below 16 px at the 640-px input scale were missed, but 15 of 100 faces at least 32 px were also missed. The five negative-image false positives were visually identified after V5 reports were sealed as a monkey, parrot, two cats and a dog; their human-face ground truth remains negative. Increasing spatial resolution could recover some small faces, but cannot by itself establish human-versus-animal discrimination or explain all larger-face misses. This hypothesis is therefore likely insufficient for face-safe approval even if latency remains acceptable.

## One fixed configuration

Use the same YuNet 2023mar weight SHA-256 `8f2383e4dd3cfbb4553ea8718107fc0423210dc964f9f4280604804ed2552fa4`, OpenCV Zoo pinned source commit `47534e27c9851bb1128ccc0102f1145e27f23f98`, directory MIT license file SHA-256 `c83b8120c50ccbd4c4f96edf53141bdd566ebb8f8e9227e415326aa1b1aba958`. No new weight is downloaded. Python executable SHA-256 `467014615a5255aca450ae88100dd2caf887da87657f00e3c2171ec44a685aec`, OpenCV 4.13.0.92 native binary SHA-256 `90034927004e4a4ebf29360480d609c8a8d2ca07c93f8b89dff86399e8534b2a`, OpenCV DNN CPU backend, two threads, OpenCL off.

For each verified CVDF JPEG, decode BGR with `IMREAD_COLOR | IMREAD_IGNORE_ORIENTATION`; require Rotation=0 and neutral EXIF. Set `scale=min(1,960/max(width,height))`, dimensions `round(width*scale),round(height*scale)`, `INTER_AREA` resize, then place at top-left of a zero-filled **960×960** BGR canvas. The detector receives exactly that canvas with `input_size=(960,960)`, score threshold **0.50**, NMS **0.30**, topK **5000**. Reverse the scale to normalized XYXY source coordinates, clip to [0,1], and match human boxes at IoU **0.50** with the existing one-to-one matcher. No horizontal/vertical flip, crop, multiscale pass, altered model weight, ensemble or confidence sweep is permitted in this protocol. The detector must never receive holdout pixels.

The detector-free V5 source audit records all 180 CVDF JPEGs with a longest side above 960 px; they are therefore downsampled, not upscaled, in this variant. The 960² canvas has **2.25×** as many pixels as the 640² V5 canvas. This is a workload estimate, not measured latency or RAM. CVDF may itself have upscaled or re-encoded an original image; a larger detector input does not recreate original detail.

## Fixed measurements and stopping rule

First run the V6 `--config-check` without `FaceDetectorYN.detect`: it must verify the V5 source-audit and V6 freeze hashes, decode the first ranked development and calibration images, and record source/canvas shapes, scale <1, and `detectorCalled=false`. Archive this check and its supervised PID/terminal state. Then evaluate **all 60 development** and **all 60 calibration** cases separately under a hard external supervisor deadline, recording all predictions, failures, denominators, per-stratum metrics, confidence bins and CPU/RSS resource spike. No failed case is silently replaced or removed; partial runs and failed resource gates remain failures. The fixed acceptance criteria remain V1/V5: >=90% image completeness per set, holdout >=30 human faces and >=10 explicit negatives, overall recall >=95%, each qualifying positive stratum recall >=90%, negative-image false-positive rate <=10%, CPU median <=150 ms/P95 <=500 ms over 30 measured runs after three warmups within 30 seconds, <=512 MiB additional peak RSS, max two execution threads. The V6 dev/calibration reports are diagnostic because the V5 outcome informed this hypothesis; neither can replace independent holdout/video/eye/final-pixel evidence.

Preserve both reports even when a gate fails. Do not choose another size, score, NMS, model or corpus after seeing V6 predictions under this protocol. Do not open the holdout until both results and hashes are reviewed. No still-image result alone authorizes `faceSafety=verified`, safe subtitle placement, cropping, W62 integration, deployment or product acceptance.
