## Selection and provenance frozen before inference

Exclude all 180 V1 selected IDs and all 76 V2 attempted IDs, including its 50 selected IDs. Official validation is partitioned into development/calibration by first SHA-256 byte of `w61-v1|validation|<image-id>` (<128 versus >=128); official test is sealed holdout. For each set, the mutually exclusive face strata and quotas remain multiple 10, lower 10, small 10, other single 10, human-verified face-negative 20. Rank candidates by SHA-256(`w61-v3|<set>|<stratum>|<image-id>`) then ID. Inspect at most the first 100 positive or 200 negative IDs per stratum. Select the first eligible quota in that order; all skipped and extra adjacent prefetched IDs are logged. Never use model outputs to choose IDs.

Perform all cheap metadata filters **before any network fetch**: exact CC BY 2.0/4.0 license, HTTPS original and landing URLs, `Rotation` numeric finite and one of 0/90/180/270, `OriginalSize` integer >0 and <=5,000,000, `OriginalMD5` valid base64 encoding of 16 bytes, and qualifying human face boxes or human-verified negative label. A blank/`nan` rotation is ineligible. For each remaining ranked candidate, require the original landing page HTTP 200 with a JSON-LD ImageObject binding that exact photo to the same CC BY license, save the exact HTML bytes/URL/timestamp/SHA, and obtain original pixels. Confirm byte length equals `OriginalSize`, binary MD5 base64 equals `OriginalMD5`, SHA-256, PIL and OpenCV decode, then rotate exactly once from encoded pixels while ignoring EXIF auto-orientation. One retry on connection timeout (30-second connect/read) is allowed. Any failure is logged before moving to the next candidate. No failed or mismatched image enters the selected sample.

The [official image-information definition](https://storage.googleapis.com/openimages/web/download_v7.html#df-image-information) defines `Rotation` as degrees counterclockwise for the original image and supplies `OriginalMD5`/`OriginalSize`; a missing rotation is unavailable information, not zero.

Runs are foreground, owner/PID recorded, at most 15 minutes with checkpoint after each ranked attempt. Resume from the same next candidate; a deadline is incomplete, not a changed sample or a corpus failure. Fetch at most two adjacent candidates at once; commit outcomes in rank order. The final manifest records all attempts, selected IDs/boxes, source/selector hashes, original page and pixel hashes, and counts. Before inference, require exactly 60 selected IDs per set, all 180 IDs disjoint from each other and excluded prior IDs, and no exact pixel SHA-256 duplicate across sets. Any duplicate is a corpus conflict, not permission to silently replace a selected ID. Different hashes do not disprove near duplicates.

Keep the holdout sealed until the complete selection manifest and its SHA-256 are frozen, source/license checks pass, and development/calibration settings are fixed. No threshold relaxation or test-set tuning. This still-image corpus does not cover blur, skin tone, eye visibility, temporal video/tracking or final MP4 placement; those remain separate W62/W67 blockers even if V3 passes. Independence from WIDER FACE training is by source only, with possible individual-photo overlap unproven.

Sources: [Open Images V7 downloads and metadata field definitions](https://storage.googleapis.com/openimages/web/download_v7.html), [Open Images V7 description](https://storage.googleapis.com/openimages/web/factsfigures_v7.html), [OpenCV Zoo pinned YuNet](https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo/tree/47534e27c9851bb1128ccc0102f1145e27f23f98/models/face_detection_yunet).
